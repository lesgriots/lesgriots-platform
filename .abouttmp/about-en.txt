LES GRIOTS is a narrative-engineering platform and a cultural infrastructure that structures, creates and passes on stories from the African continent and its diasporas, on screen, on stage and on the page. Storytelling is the world’s most widely shared tradition: every culture has practiced it; ours made it a charge, that of the griot. The house carries that gesture forward with today’s tools, whatever the format, whatever the medium.

Driven by one mission, to reveal powerful, untold stories and the women and men who carry them, LES GRIOTS gives creators the power to tell: fresh perspectives on timeless stories, universal truths, big and small, that speak to us wherever we are; stories that break through barriers, heal, transform and connect us to one another, and forge those who carry them. Because new stories need new faces, the house is devoted to the generation creating now and the one to come, so that everyone can build their story, carry it far, and make a living from it.

Great stories do more than entertain: they move people, and shape culture.
